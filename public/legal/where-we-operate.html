Where we can operate

Two different things decide whether byoutoyou can take a booking in a state: whether a licensed professional may work outside a salon there, and whether they can be engaged as an independent contractor. A state stays closed until both are answered. Every hospital stays listed either way — you can always join a waitlist.

California Not operating ABC test California applies the ABC test broadly. Prong B asks whether the work is outside the usual course of the hiring entity's business — bedside beauty visits are precisely byoutoyou's usual course, so a contractor classification is very hard to defend here. Employ through a staffing partner or stay out.
