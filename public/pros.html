What we ask

  • A current cosmetology, barbering, esthetics or nail licence in the state where you would work. Give us the number — we check it against your state board's public register ourselves, so there is nothing to photograph.
  • Your own liability insurance, $1M per occurrence. Your insurer can email the certificate straight to us.
  • A background check, run before your first visit by a licensed screening provider who verifies your identity directly. We never ask you to send a photo of an ID, a passport or a Social Security card — if any platform asks you to email those, that is your cue to stop.
  • Personal care only — no clinical work of any kind, and the unit's answer is always final.

What we do not ask

  • No exclusivity, no minimum, no shifts. Decline anything, for any reason, with no penalty.
  • No non-compete and no non-solicitation. A family you meet through us is yours to keep working with, on the platform or off it.
  • No travel outside the radius you declare. We will never offer you a hospital you cannot reach.
